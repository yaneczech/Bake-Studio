# License FAQ

This FAQ explains the intent of the Bake Studio license in plain language. It is a convenience document only. The actual license terms are in [LICENSE](/Users/janjanecek/Documents/GitHub/VJB/Bake%20Studio/LICENSE).

## Can I use Bake Studio in commercial work?

Yes. You may use Bake Studio in paid artistic, studio, client, agency, broadcast, installation, or production work.

## Can I make money from videos or outputs created with Bake Studio?

Yes. You may sell performances, visuals, rendered outputs, installations, services, or other work made using Bake Studio.

## Can I use Bake Studio inside my company?

Yes. Internal business use is allowed.

## Can I modify Bake Studio for my own workflow?

Yes. You may modify the software for your own use.

## Can I share my modified version with someone else?

Yes, as long as you share it free of charge, keep the license text, keep the copyright notice, and clearly state what you changed.

## Can I sell Bake Studio itself?

No. You may not sell the software, charge a license fee for it, or resell copies of it.

## Can I put Bake Studio in a paid bundle, marketplace listing, or product download?

No, if payment is required to obtain Bake Studio itself.

## Can I charge for installation, setup, customization, support, or training?

Yes. Paid services around Bake Studio are allowed, as long as the payment is for the service and not for a license to the software itself.

## Can I host Bake Studio as part of a paid service?

The current license intends to allow paid services performed using Bake Studio, but hosted-service and SaaS-style use can be legally nuanced. If this becomes an important distribution model, the license text should be reviewed and possibly clarified.

## Can I ship Bake Studio preinstalled on hardware and charge for the hardware?

This is a gray area under the current wording if access to the software itself is part of the paid offer. If this matters commercially, the license should be clarified before relying on it.

## Can I fork Bake Studio and sell my fork?

No. Forks and modified versions may be shared only free of charge.

## Can I relicense my modified version under another license?

No, not in a way that restricts the rights granted by the Bake Studio license to recipients.

## Is this open source?

No. This is a source-available license, not an OSI open-source license.
